# Phone-vision roadmap

The master sequence is gate-driven:

1. phone camera and ray-space contract;
2. polarity-preserving frontend;
3. canonical direction and retinotopic field;
4. LPi/LPTC and competitive-disinhibition ablations;
5. analytic robust SO(3);
6. normal-FOV observability diagnostic;
7. orientation and angular feature memory;
8. oracle-only rotation compensation and scale-free translation;
9. oracle-pose topological scene memory;
10. streaming API and export feasibility.

Failed gates do not become stable capabilities. Downstream implementation may
continue in oracle_only mode, but claims and releases remain gated by evidence.
